## What I did

- Co-designed and built a character-led, Y2K-inspired portfolio that tells a 5-stop scroll-driven story across East Asia.
- Led the visual identity: pastel palette, four-font Japanese-inspired type system and layout, plus the character, story and copywriting.
- Designed and animated the interactions with GSAP: a draggable lantern wish-collecting mechanic, a playable snack-catching mini game, and in-browser sound effects with music and mode toggles.
- Art-directed AI-assisted watercolour illustrations through prompts, then cut out and cleaned up the assets.
